Pricing FAQ

What counts as a minute?+
Each minute of active AI conversation counts as one minute. Hold time, spam calls, and robocalls are never counted.
What happens if I go over my minutes?+
Calls continue to be answered — we never drop a call. Overage is billed at $0.09/minute and shown clearly on your invoice. We'll send you an alert before you hit 80% usage.
Is there a setup fee?+
No setup fees, ever. The 14-day free trial requires no credit card. You only pay when you choose a plan.
Can I switch plans?+
Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately. Prorated credits apply when downgrading.
What's included in the free trial?+
The 14-day trial gives you full access to the Professional plan — appointment booking, lead capture, CRM integrations, and 300 minutes. No credit card required.
Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

How quickly can I be up and running?+
Most businesses are live within 5 minutes of signing up. Create your account, enter your business info, and forward your phone number. That's it.
Do I need to change my business phone number?+
No. Your customers keep calling your existing number. You simply set call forwarding on your phone so calls route to your Halla AI number. This takes about 60 seconds and works with any carrier.
Does the AI really sound natural?+
Yes. Halla AI uses the latest conversational AI with a warm, professional voice. The vast majority of callers don't realize they're talking to AI. You can listen to sample calls before you sign up.
Can I customize what the AI says?+
Absolutely. You control the greeting, the AI's name, its tone, the questions it asks, how it handles different call types, and what it does with the information it collects. All from your dashboard.

Features

Can the AI book appointments in my actual calendar?+
Yes. Halla AI integrates natively with Google Calendar, Outlook, Calendly, and Acuity. It checks real-time availability and books confirmed slots. No double bookings.
What languages does Halla AI support?+
English and Spanish are included on every plan. Enterprise plans support up to 5 languages including French, Portuguese, and Mandarin. The AI auto-detects the caller's language.
Will I be charged for spam calls?+
Never. Spam and robocalls are detected within seconds and terminated. They are not charged against your minutes, ever. This is included free on all plans.
Can the AI transfer calls to me?+
Yes. You can set rules for when the AI should attempt a warm transfer to your phone, a team member, or a department. Available on Professional and above.

Privacy & Compliance

Is Halla AI HIPAA compliant?+
Halla AI can operate in HIPAA-aware mode for medical and dental practices. We sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for applicable plans. Contact sales for details.
Are my calls recorded and stored?+
Call recordings and transcripts are stored securely and accessible in your dashboard. You control the retention period. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
Do callers know they're talking to AI?+
You have full control. You can configure the AI to disclose that it's an automated assistant, or not. We recommend following applicable local disclosure laws in your state.
