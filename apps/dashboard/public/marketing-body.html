How to Handle Emergency Calls with an AI Receptionist

Emergency calls require speed, accuracy, and empathy. Here's how to configure your AI to handle them flawlessly.

Published March 1, 2025 · 5 min read

Picture this: It's 2:00 AM. A customer's pipe just burst. Water is flooding their office. They call your number, panicked. Who answers?

If you have Halla AI, your AI receptionist answers immediately — not with a robotic script, but with a calm, empathetic voice that collects critical details and dispatches help instantly.

The difference between an answered emergency call and a missed one:
A missed emergency call can mean thousands of dollars in lost revenue, a damaged reputation, and a customer who never calls again. An answered emergency call means you dispatch a technician, save the customer's property, and earn their loyalty for life.

Step 1: Define What "Emergency" Means for Your Business

Not every after-hours call is an emergency. Before you configure your AI, define exactly what constitutes an emergency:

  • For clinics: Severe pain, heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty. Route to your duty doctor or direct the caller to local emergency services
  • For facilities and AC maintenance: No cooling in extreme heat, water leaks, power outage, lift entrapment
  • For property managers: Flooding, fire-alarm activation, security incidents
  • For hotels: Guest safety issues, room-access problems, serious maintenance faults

Pro Tip: Create a list of "red flag" keywords for your industry. The AI will use these to automatically escalate calls. For example: "flooding," "smoke," "bleeding," "seizure," "gas leak".

Step 2: Configure Your AI's Emergency Call Flow

During onboarding, you'll set up your emergency call flow. Here's what a typical emergency flow looks like:

  1. Answer with urgency: "I'm Halla AI, the AI dispatcher for [Your Business]. Are you reporting an emergency?"
  2. Collect critical details: "What's the problem? Where are you? Is there immediate danger?"
  3. Flag and escalate: "I'm dispatching this as an emergency. Your on-call technician will contact you within 5 minutes."
  4. Notify your team: The AI emails your on-call team with the job summary — address, problem description, customer name, and phone number.

Mobile Carriers (GCC-Wide)

Good news — one set of codes works everywhere. Every major GCC carrier runs on the international GSM standard, so the same forwarding codes work whether you're on stc, Mobily, Zain, e&, du, Ooredoo, Vodafone Qatar, Batelco, Omantel, or Asiacell. Just dial the code below on your phone's keypad.

Two ways to forward: Unconditional sends every call straight to Halla AI — your phone never rings. Conditional only forwards when you don't pick up, your line is busy, or your phone is off — your phone rings first. Most businesses prefer conditional so they can still answer personally when available.

*62*number# activate

#62# cancel

Tip: ##002# cancels all conditional forwarding at once.

Codes can vary slightly by device, plan, or roaming status. If a code doesn't activate, contact your carrier's support line or check their app — most GCC carriers also let you manage forwarding from their official mobile app.

Business VoIP or PBX System

If your business uses a cloud phone system or PBX rather than individual mobile lines, forwarding is configured in your provider's admin portal rather than with a dial code:

  1. Log in to your phone system's admin portal
  2. Find the call handling or forwarding settings for the number you want to connect
  3. Add your Halla AI number as a forwarding destination
  4. Choose unconditional (always forward) or conditional (forward when unanswered/busy) depending on how you want calls handled
  5. If conditional, set a ring time before forwarding (10–15 seconds is typical)
  6. Save changes and test with a call to your business number

Exact menu names vary by provider. If you're not sure where this setting lives, our support team can walk you through your specific system.

Troubleshooting

Q: Calls are going to voicemail instead of Halla AI

A: Your carrier may have a shorter ring timeout. Set your forward time to 10–15 seconds.

Q: I hear two greetings (carrier voicemail + Halla AI)

A: Disable your carrier's voicemail. Contact your provider to turn off the default mailbox.

Q: Forwarding works but caller ID shows my own number

A: This is normal. Your carrier sends the original caller ID inside the call metadata. Halla AI can see the real caller.

Q: Do these codes work while roaming?

A: Usually yes, but international roaming can sometimes disable call forwarding. If a code fails while abroad, set it up before you travel.
