## How connecting works (same for every app)

1. **Pick your app** — during setup or on **Integrations** in the dashboard  
2. **Connect** — either **“Connect with [App]”** (one click) or follow the numbered steps and paste what we ask for  
3. **We test automatically** — you’ll see “Lead created in [App]” or “Connected successfully”  
4. **Done** — new callers sync without anything else to configure  

**Skip anytime** — you can connect later from **Integrations**.

## One click vs guided (what you’ll see)

| Label in Call IQ | What you do |
|------------------|-------------|
| **One click** badge | Click Connect → sign in on the app’s website → approve → return here |
| **No badge (guided)** | Open settings (button provided) → copy one value at a time → Connect |

You never need to know what an API, webhook, or Zapier is on the main path.
