GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Q: What about GDPR and CCPA?
Halla AI is GDPR and CCPA compliant. You can request data export or deletion at any time by emailing hello@hallaai.com.
Data Subject Rights: Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your personal data. Contact hello@hallaai.com to exercise these rights.

Website Disclaimer

Q: Do I need a disclaimer on my website?
Yes, we recommend it. You should add a line to your website footer or contact page:

"Calls are answered by Halla AI, an AI receptionist. By calling us, you agree to speak with an automated system."

Our AI already discloses itself on every call, so you are covered either way, but the website notice adds extra transparency.

Troubleshooting & Edge Cases

What if the AI accidentally books a duplicate appointment? +
The AI checks your calendar via integration before booking. If a time slot is already taken, it will not book it. Duplicates are extremely rare. If one occurs, you can cancel it manually, and we will credit you 10 minutes of overage.
Can the AI handle angry or aggressive callers? +
Yes. The AI is trained to remain calm, polite, and de-escalate. If a caller becomes abusive, the AI will say:
"I'm sorry you're upset. I'm an AI, so I can't get angry. I'll take a message and have a human call you back within one hour. Is that okay?"
If the caller continues to yell, the AI will politely end the call and send you an urgent SMS.
Do you support call recording? +
Yes, but only with caller consent. The AI will say: "This call may be recorded for quality and training purposes." If the caller says no, the AI does not record. Recordings are stored for 30 days.
What happens if my internet goes down? +
Halla AI runs entirely in the cloud. Your internet does not matter. Calls route through our servers. As long as your phone number is forwarded to us or ported to us, calls will still be answered even if your office loses power or internet.

One-Party Consent vs. All-Party Consent

In the United States, call recording laws fall into two categories:

Type Definition Number of States
One-Party Consent Only one person in the conversation needs to know the call is being recorded 38 states + DC
All-Party Consent Every person in the conversation must consent to the recording 12 states

If you have Halla AI, your AI receptionist answers immediately — not with a robotic script, but with a calm, empathetic voice that collects critical details and dispatches help instantly.

The difference between an answered emergency call and a missed one:
A missed emergency call can mean thousands of dollars in lost revenue, a damaged reputation, and a customer who never calls again. An answered emergency call means you dispatch a technician, save the customer's property, and earn their loyalty for life.

Step 1: Define What "Emergency" Means for Your Business

Not every after-hours call is an emergency. Before you configure your AI, define exactly what constitutes an emergency:

  • For plumbers: Burst pipes, active flooding, gas smells, no hot water
  • For electricians: Sparking outlets, burning smells, complete power outage, shock hazards
  • For HVAC: No heat in freezing weather, no AC in extreme heat, gas smells
  • For veterinarians: Bleeding, seizures, breathing difficulty, toxin ingestion

Pro Tip: Create a list of "red flag" keywords for your industry. The AI will use these to automatically escalate calls. For example: "flooding," "smoke," "bleeding," "seizure," "gas leak".

Step 2: Configure Your AI's Emergency Call Flow

During onboarding, you'll set up your emergency call flow. Here's what a typical emergency flow looks like:

1
Answer with urgency: "I'm Halla AI, the AI dispatcher for [Your Business]. Are you reporting an emergency?"
2
Collect critical details: "What's the problem? Where are you? Is there immediate danger?"
3
Flag and escalate: "I'm dispatching this as an emergency. Your on-call technician will contact you within 5 minutes."
4
Notify your team: The AI sends an SMS to your on-call technician with the full job summary — address, problem description, customer name, and phone number.

Step 3: Set Up Your On-Call Notification System

The AI is only as good as its notification system. Here's how to set up your emergency notifications:

  • Primary contact: Your on-call technician's phone number (SMS and call)
  • Backup contact: A secondary number if the primary doesn't respond within 3 minutes
  • Email summary: A detailed email with the full transcript sent to your dispatch team
  • Slack notification: Send a message to your #emergencies channel (via Zapier)
Example SMS: " EMERGENCY: Burst pipe at 1428 Oak Street. Customer reports active flooding in basement. On-call plumber: Please call (555) 847-2931 immediately. Status: Dispatching now."

Step 4: Test Your Emergency Flow

Before you go live, test your emergency flow with a few mock calls. Here's what to test:

  • Does the AI recognize your emergency keywords?
  • Does the AI capture the address and problem description correctly?
  • Does the notification SMS arrive within seconds of the call ending?
  • Does the backup contact work if the primary doesn't respond?

Common mistake: Many businesses forget to test their emergency flow with a real simulated call. Don't wait for a real emergency to find out your notifications aren't working. Test it now.

The Bottom Line

Emergency calls are the most important calls your business receives. A single missed emergency call can mean a lost customer, a bad review, or worse — property damage or even injury. With Halla AI, you never miss an emergency call again.
